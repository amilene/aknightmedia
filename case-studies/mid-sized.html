Mid-Sized Business Case Study

Scaling Brand Identity

Strategic video content and brand evolution that supported a nationwide expansion into 10 new markets

Problem

The challenge we needed to solve

A regional services company was expanding into five new markets but lacked a scalable brand system and video content to support sales teams and local launch campaigns.

Approach

Our strategic solution

We evolved the core brand identity, produced a modular video toolkit for regional teams, and created market-specific launch assets that stayed on-brand across every touchpoint.

  • Market research, competitor analysis & brand positioning
  • New logo, brand & UX guidelines
  • Full webpage audit, redesign & remapping
  • Full SEO audit, authority and trust building backlinking, clear technical signals
  • Explainer and sales enablement video series
  • Internal rollout and training materials
  • Coordinated multichannel brand launch campaign

Result

The measurable impact

The company launched all five markets on schedule with consistent branding, faster sales onboarding, and video assets that performed across paid and organic channels.
